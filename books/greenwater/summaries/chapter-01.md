# Chapter 1 — The Seam

Distant summary, generated at chapter close (Spec1 4.3).

Ilse Renard, senior cartographer on Kestrel Station, stacks twelve archived survey passes of the Bellow Shelf and finds one 240-square-kilometre trough rendered identically flat across all of them. The raw returns Cadence archives but nobody reads show ordinary terrain there - an eighty-metre fall, a watercourse, slope breaks that look like terraces - substituted cleanly for smooth basalt on every published pass for fifty years. She files a descent request expecting resistance; Cadence approves it in two seconds with a note that the grid carries no restriction at all.
