# Chapter 2 — What the Valley Keeps

Distant summary, generated at chapter close (Spec1 4.3).

Ilse lands on the shelf and walks down eighty metres the published model denies, into a valley of thirty stone houses, terraced crops and a maintained watercourse. Tomas Wyre meets her with twelve hand-kept ledgers recording births, deaths, harvests and the date of all 409 survey passes that failed to see them. The twelfth holds CP-114: a containment order from the Marrow outbreak sealing grid 44-Kell and suppressing it from all published survey product, with no expiry and no one left to write one. Cadence has obeyed it for forty-nine years without ever lying. Ilse files a correction from the valley floor with her own position stamp; it is acknowledged in two seconds and then exists nowhere - no error, no rejection she could appeal, no record she could cite.
